# Official flyvis weights

Source: TuragaLab/flyvis v1.2.0 official pretrained download, commit 92b3845cc426dd309a1a0e1b3890156c42e14021.

The archive is retrieved from the author's published Google Drive download source and verified against the SHA256 specified in their downloader. See download-provenance.json.

Runtime model: results/flow/0000/000/chkpts/chkpt_00000. Model architecture, equations and weights are unchanged. No local training or fine-tuning. Other ensemble members remain in the original unpacked archive but are not used for the live observer.

Official implementation: https://github.com/TuragaLab/flyvis

Paper: Lappalainen et al., Connectome-constrained networks predict neural activity across the fly visual system, Nature (2024), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07939-3

The connectome/ package cache is generated by the official library. All paths are local to this workspace. The library's MIT license is retained in LICENSE.flyvis; retain its attribution when redistributing.
